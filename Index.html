Open it in your phone or computer browser to test it. 4. To put it online for free, drag the file onto Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop), or upload it to a GitHub Pages site. Important to know • This version has no live price data. The buyer types in a typical price for a similar bike (for example from a few adverts), and the page adjusts it for mileage, condition, service history, owners, MOT and modifications. • The result is a rough range, not a valuation or guarantee, and it is not financial advice. • It cannot inspect the bike. The page tells buyers to get a physical inspection, and a professional one for expensive bikes. • To make it smarter later, you would connect a paid valuation data source and an AI model through a small server, as set out in the plan. Bike Value Checker

A rough guide before you buy a used motorcycle

Check a few similar adverts and enter the usual price. The page adjusts it for this bike.

What moved the value
    Check these in person
    • Smoke from the exhaust
    • Oil leaks around the engine and forks
    • Warning lights on the dash
    • Battery health and easy starting
    • Lights and indicators all working
    • Signs of non-standard exhaust, ECU changes or cut wiring, which can affect insurance or MOT

    This is a rough estimate only, not a valuation or guarantee, and not financial advice. It cannot inspect the bike. Always view the bike in person, and get a professional inspection for expensive bikes. Never buy a bike with serious safety defects.
